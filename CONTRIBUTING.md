# Contributing

This project is **under development** and is licensed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 or later** (see `LICENSE`).

## What the licence means for you

- You may use, study, build and modify the source code, documents and designs.
- If you distribute a modified version (source, designs, firmware or a built device that includes this code), you must also provide your complete source under the same GPL licence.
- The GPL cannot force you to send changes to this repository. We ask that you do: improvements that stay in private forks help nobody else.

## How to contribute changes

1. Open an issue to discuss larger changes first.
2. Fork the repository, make your change on a branch, and open a pull request to `main`.
3. Keep documents honest: mark claims as CONFIRMED / PROPOSED / OPEN, cite sources, and never invent part numbers, prices, stock or datasheet values.
4. By submitting a pull request you agree that your contribution is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later, the same as the project.

## Ground rules

- Lawful use only: no jamming or blocking functions, and transmit features must respect licence-exempt bands and power limits.
- Keep the India-only sourcing rule in mind when proposing parts, or flag when a part is import-only.
- Regulatory notes are not legal advice.
