## BIS compulsory registration (CRS)
- Electronics and Information Technology Goods (Requirement of Compulsory Registration) Order, 2021, S.O. 1248(E), 18 Mar 2021 (LAW) https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Electronics-and-Information-Technology-Goods-Requirement-of-Compulsory-Registration-Order-2021-1.pdf . Goods in the Schedule must conform to the Indian Standard and bear the Standard Mark under a BIS licence (Scheme-II). Carve-outs: export goods conforming to the foreign buyer's specification; goods with a specific exemption letter. Amendments: S.O. 2844(E) 1 Jul 2021 (deferral, 3-month window to mark at port, Highly Specialised Equipment exemption), later ones listed (1929(E) 2023, 1652(E) 2024, 4378(E) 2024, 1363(E) 2025, 4362(E) 2025, 352(E) 2026) not individually read; S.O. 4997(E) (29 Oct 2025) replaces IS 13252 Pt 1:2010 and IS 616:2017 with IS/IEC 62368 Pt 1:2023 (parallel until 1 Nov 2028) and adds item 65 (extended-reality products); S.O. 1246(E) 10 Mar 2026 rewrote the HSE exemption (< 100 units per model per year plus technical criteria).
- Schedule items of interest: 1 electronic games, 2 laptops/tablets, 15 automatic data processing machines, 16/17 power adaptors, 20 sealed secondary Li cells/batteries for portable use (IS 16046 Pt 2:2018), 23 mobile phones, 30 power banks, 44 smart watches. **No entry names a modular handheld**; which item (if any) applies: could not determine.
- GUIDANCE (CRS FAQ modified 28 Sept 2022; predates the 2025–26 amendments): integral sealed cells/batteries need registration if the host is also covered (Q43) and not if the host is not (Q66); batteries and adaptors are independently registered (Q40, Q44); the Order applies to finished goods, not components (Q34); not applicable to goods imported for demonstration, R&D or testing (Q35; underlying circular W-47/4/2020-IPHW of 26.04.2022 not opened, no unit cap seen). No small-quantity domestic exemption found.

## TEC MTCTE
Phase III/IV notification No. 5-2/2021-TC/TEC/93, 22.09.2021 (LAW) http://tec.gov.in/pdf/MTCTE/Notification_MTCTE_3_4.pdf lists: base station, repeater, compact cellular network, smart/CCTV camera, smart watch, smart electricity meter, tracking device, IoT gateway, end point device for environmental monitoring, "Equipment Operating in 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Band". Smart watch, mobile handset, server, smart camera and PoS devices were exempted by S.O. 2372(E), 24 May 2022 https://www.tec.gov.in/pdf/MTCTE/Exemption%20of%20five%20items.pdf . Phase V (5G etc.) and VI (S.O. 982(E), 25 Feb 2025: cellular CPE, fixed wireless phones, IP security equipment, NGSO terminals) are irrelevant. The essential-requirement text defining the 2.4/5 GHz category was not obtained, so whether a Wi-Fi/Bluetooth handheld is in scope: could not determine. No Bluetooth, NFC or sub-GHz category found. TEC helpdesk: help.mtcte.tec@gov.in.

## Battery shipping
India-specific carriage rules (DGCA, PESO): not found. IATA 2026 guidance (snippets): Li-ion in equipment UN 3481, packing instruction 967 Section II for cells ≤ 20 Wh and batteries ≤ 100 Wh; all cells must pass UN 38.3. A 3000–3500 mAh cell at 3.6–3.7 V is about 11–13 Wh.

## E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022
G.S.R. 801(E), 2 Nov 2022, effective 1 Apr 2023. Producer (Rule 3(t), via CPCB SOP) includes anyone manufacturing and offering equipment under their own brand. Registration on the CPCB portal (five-year validity; fees start at ₹2,500 for < 50 MT targets). Rule 2(c) (secondary copy): the Rules do not apply to micro enterprises under the MSMED Act (an ACMA FAQ describes this more narrowly; not reconciled). Schedule I (copy cut off partway) names laptops, tablets, phones, GPS, routers; no handheld-device row seen. Battery Waste Management Rules 2022 are separate; whether they bind a maker with a fitted cell: could not determine.

## Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011
Rule 6 declarations: manufacturer name and address (importer and country of origin for imports), generic name, net quantity, month and year of manufacture, MRP inclusive of taxes, consumer-complaint contact. E-commerce listings must show the same (Rule 6(10)). Packages for industrial/institutional consumers excluded. QR-code relief status: could not determine.

## Prototypes and small batches
