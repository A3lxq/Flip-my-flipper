# Research reports

Condensed versions of the reports produced by research agents during the project (2026-11-01). They are **not verbatim**: tables and key facts are kept, long passages are shortened. Every report shares these limits:

- Facts were gathered from web pages and datasheets by automated research agents. Treat all of it as unverified until checked against the primary document.
- Stock and prices are snapshots. Several sellers (Robu 403, Robocraze 429, Thingbits, Rhydolabz, Silverline, Tanna TechBiz) could not be opened or were not searched. Evelta often shows contradictory stock labels ("in stock" beside "Sold Out").
- DigiKey India, Mouser India, Tanotis, LCSC and overseas sellers count as imports under the project's India-only rule. "Not found" never means "import-only".
- Regulatory reports are not legal advice. `dot.gov.in`, India Code and the e-Gazette were mostly unreadable; many items rest on mirrors, law-firm summaries and news.
