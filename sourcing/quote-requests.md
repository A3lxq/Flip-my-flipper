## 7. What a good answer looks like

- A written statement of quantity in stock in India, the price including GST and the delivery time.
- The source of the parts (manufacturer or authorised distributor) and an invoice or datecode for chips.
- Anything that says "ordered from overseas" means the part counts as an **import** under the project rule, even if the listing looks Indian.
- Record the answer as CONFIRMED only when it is in writing; otherwise leave the item OPEN in `01-requirements-and-architecture.md`.
